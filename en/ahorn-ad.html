Cooking pleasure like at home

The alcove model with double bed, the Canada AD, impresses with a large high-quality kitchenette and plenty of sleeping and seating space. With extensive worktop space, numerous drawers and cupboards for storing kitchen utensils, this kitchen is a dream for all hobby cooks. The Canada AD is designed for six people with dinette conversion. The bed in the rear offers a pleasant lying comfort and creates space for luggage due to the elevation. The alcove double bed can be folded up and thus offers an open feeling of space during the day. An additional wardrobe offers the possibility to remove shirts and clothes from the living area and store them carefully.

GENERAL TERMS FOR MOTORHOME RENTAL

By signing General Terms, both parties, lessee and lessor confirm that they have accepted the agreement and are committed to respect them.

1. Lease conditions
A motorhome can be leased by person:
who has a personal document with picture proving that he/she is older than 21
has at least a 3- year valid driver’s licence for category B vehicle

2. Lease reservation and payment
After confirmation of a lease date, lessor sends lessee an estimate valid for three days. Lessee is obliged to pay 50% of value within 3 days of receiving an estimate, thus confirming the lease reservation date. Lessee will pay the remaining amount of lease no later than 14 days before scheduled date of taking the vehicle. For reservations that are confirmed less than 30 days before taking the vehicle, lessee is obliged to pay in advance the full amount 3 days after receiving the estimate. If lessee does not make the payment in aforementioned time limit, the reservation will be cancelled.

3. Price
The price of the lease is charged according to the price list published on the lessor’s web site and it depends on the vehicle and the date of the lease.
The price includes:
renting of the vehicle on agreed date
compulsory and casco insurance with 1% deductible (percentage of lessee in damage) as well as additional insurance for roadside assistance
basic equipment: power cable, water hose with adapter, bike rack
For each lease, lessee must pay fixed expenses of 125 EUR, which include expenses for issuance and reception of vehicle, toilet chemicals, gas, biodegradable toilet paper, external and internal vehicle cleaning.
A 2000 EUR deposit has to be paid for the duration of the lease, and it will be returned on the last day of the lease, after handing over the undamaged vehicle.
RECEPTION OF THE VEHICLE
Vehicles can be picked up in Samobor between 10 and 14 h. It is also possible to deliver the vehicles to all locations in Croatia and Western Europe.
Late return of the vehicle is additionally charged by agreed contract.
